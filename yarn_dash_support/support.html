Frequently asked

Does YarnDash need an internet connection?
No — it works completely offline. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.


Do I need an account?
No accounts, no sign-in. Your high scores are saved only on your own device.


Why did my controls reverse?
That's the yellow gem's "dizzy" effect — it wears off on its own after a few seconds.
